--- cover letter body ---

Adds riscv64-softmmu to --target-list and generates the machine-version
and CPU-model metadata for it, mirroring x86_64 and aarch64. Building this
target surfaced three real, build-breaking bugs in the existing
debian/parse-*.pl scripts and one blob-purging bug that meant no riscv64
guest could ever boot - see the individual commit messages for each.

Patch 1/4 (the OpenSBI purge fix) is arguably independent of the other
three and could land alone if reviewers prefer, but it only matters once
riscv64-softmmu is actually built, so it's included here rather than sent
separately.

Verified: built this tree with riscv64-softmmu in the target list, ran
the resulting qemu-system-riscv64 against a real Proxmox VE install, and
confirmed a riscv64 guest boots OpenSBI and EDK2 UEFI firmware to the
boot manager (needs the corresponding qemu-server/pve-edk2-firmware
changes from the follow-up series to actually configure such a guest;
this series alone just gets the binary and its metadata built).

Patches:
  1/4 fix: purge only the riscv32 OpenSBI blob, not riscv64's
  2/4 debian/parse-cpu-models.pl: recognize riscv64 CPU models
  3/4 debian/parse-machines.pl: don't die when a target has no versioned machines
  4/4 debian/rules: build riscv64-softmmu and generate its machine/CPU-model metadata
